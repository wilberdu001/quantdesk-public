02 / CORRECTNESS BEFORE PERFORMANCE

Designed to reject
unearned returns.

01

Use the executable side

A buy starts at ask. A sell starts at bid. Last-trade prices do not stand in for missing quotes.

02

Make failure explicit

Stale, delayed, crossed or incomplete quotes produce a rejection, not a fabricated fill.

03

Conserve the accounting

The spread, slippage and fees reconcile to the cash loss. Available quantity is a bound, not a fill guarantee.
